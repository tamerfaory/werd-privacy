Werd Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Werd is designed to provide Islamic worship and reference features while respecting user privacy.

Information We Collect

Werd does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information from users.

Local App Data

Preferences, progress, and other app settings may be stored locally on your device so the app can remember your choices and progress. This information is not transmitted to Werd or stored on Werd-controlled servers.

Analytics and Advertising

Werd does not use third-party advertising networks or analytics services to track users.

Accounts

Werd does not require users to create an account to use the app.

Children's Privacy

Werd does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Third-Party Content and Links

The app may include religious reference content or links to external resources. If you choose to open an external website, that website's own privacy practices and policies apply.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If Werd's privacy practices change in a future version, this policy may be updated to reflect those changes.

Contact

For privacy questions or support related to Werd, please contact the app developer through the support information provided on Werd's App Store listing.
